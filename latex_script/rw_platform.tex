Social media platforms differ in structural affordances that systematically
shape user behavior. Boulianne et al.~\cite{boulianne2025} distinguish platforms
by their threading architecture, character limits, anonymity provisions, and
voting mechanisms, and show that these structural features predict how and why
people engage politically on different services. Shugars and Ha~\cite{shugars2025}
compared Twitter and Reddit directly, finding that Reddit's \emph{conversational
visibility} - the ability for users to see and engage with exchanges as a
whole - enabled more cross-cutting and productive political discourse than
Twitter's broadcast-oriented design. The present study extends this
platform-comparative framing to YouTube, which shares neither Reddit's threading
nor Twitter's follow-graph, and applies it to a substantially larger corpus than
prior comparisons have used.
