  used in the manuscript for:
      Echo Chambers and Partisan Polarization The homophily principle -
      that similar people interact at higher rates than dissimilar ones
      - underpins computational echo-chamber research.

      Crucially, homophily is a property of a network, so we measure it
      on an explicit user-user reply graph and report Newman's
      assortativity coefficient for categorical attributes alongside a
      chance-corrected homophily index; raw same-stance rates are
      uninterpretable without the baseline expected under random mixing.

      Because that share depends on how common each stance is among
      reply targets, we report it against the value expected under
      random mixing and summarise the whole graph with Newman's
      assortativity coefficient , where 0 is random mixing and negative
      values indicate cross-stance interaction.

      Since homophily is defined on networks , the reply-graph measure
      is the appropriate one, and it indicates that Reddit in this
      corpus functions as a contested arena rather than a set of sealed
      chambers.

SOURCES CHANGED TO MAKE THE EVIDENCE CHECKABLE

  Five references were either unreadable without a subscription or
  were grey literature. All five are gone.

  isd2023 + isd2023muslim (web)  ->  hofmann2026 + rosen2025
      The two Institute for Strategic Dialogue dispatches were the
      only non-peer-reviewed sources in the paper. Replaced with
      Hofmann et al. 2026 (Springer LNISO 257-272, free preprint on
      arXiv), which annotates 4,983 YouTube comments on this exact
      conflict using the same three stance labels we use, and Rosen
      and Walther 2025 (Social Media + Society, gold open access) on
      the post-7-October rise in anti-Jewish and anti-Muslim posting.
      The 50-fold and 43-fold figures were dropped with them: those
      were ISD's own measurements and no peer-reviewed study reports
      them, so the claim now rests on what the new sources state.
      Hofmann was also added to Related Work, where it belonged
      anyway as the closest prior work to our design.


  krippendorff2004 (book)  ->  hayes2007
      Hayes and Krippendorff 2007, Communication Methods and Measures
      1(1):77-89. Free from UPenn Annenberg, and the standard journal
      citation for the alpha statistic we report.

  leeseung1999 (Nature)    ->  leeseung2000
      Lee and Seung, Algorithms for Non-negative Matrix Factorization,
      NeurIPS 2000. Free from papers.nips.cc, and the better citation
      because we use the algorithms, not the Nature letter's argument.

  sunstein2017 (book)      ->  removed
      Cited once for a framing claim that bail2018 already supports,
      and bail2018 is stored here in full text with the finding
      highlighted. Nothing in the paper now depends on it.

  mcpherson2001 (paywalled)  ->  covered by newman2003
      Newman 2003 states the same principle formally and on
      networks: 'assortative mixing in networks, the tendency for
      vertices to be connected to other vertices that are like (or
      unlike) them'. That is the better citation here anyway,
      because we measure assortativity on a reply graph rather than
      sociological homophily. Newman is free on arXiv and held here
      in full text. Cinelli 2021, also held in full text,
      independently shows homophily underpinning echo-chamber work.

  santiago2025 (paywalled)  ->  guerra2025
      Santiago et al. was a subscription-only Springer chapter with
      no open copy anywhere. Replaced by Guerra, Lepre and Karakus
      2025, Natural Language Processing Journal 11:100156, free
      preprint on arXiv 2412.10913. Also Reddit, also this conflict,
      also affect-focused, and far larger: 450,000 posts across four
      subreddits against Santiago's 80-day window.

  flesch1948 (paywalled)    ->  analysis dropped
      Nothing could replace it: 06_revision/core_stats.py hardcodes
      Flesch's own constants (206.835, 1.015, 84.6), so no other
      work can be cited for that formula, and a 1948 paper has no
      open version. The readability result it supported was one
      descriptive line (Reddit 63.7 vs YouTube 67.1) that the
      manuscript itself already discounted as 'not evidence of
      narrative complexity or argumentative depth', with negligible
      stance-level differences. That line was removed. The comment
      length contrast it sat beside, which is the load-bearing part,
      needs no citation and is unchanged.
